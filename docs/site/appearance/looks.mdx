A theme sets the colours of the page and its glass; a backdrop puts folds of colour behind the glass; frost, drift and grain tune the backdrop. None of them reads right alone — a backdrop takes its lightness from the theme, the theme's tint veils the backdrop, a light page wants a pale set behind it — so the thing you pick, keep and send is the pair with its tuning: a **look**.

**Settings → Look** is the one section for all of it (⌘, then *Look* in the rail; Settings reopens where you left it). The looks are tiles at the top, each a small picture of the window — the folds, and a chip of the page on them. Under them, a pill switches between the two halves of the look on screen: **The page** (the [theme](/appearance/themes), its tiles and its editor) and **Behind the glass** (the [backdrop](/appearance/backdrop), its colours, drift, frost and grain). Change either and the look's tile lets go — the line beside the pill says so — and **Save this look…** keeps the new pairing under a name of its own, in *Mine*.

View → Look, and *Look: …* in the ⌘K command bar, apply a look from anywhere. ⌘Z in Settings takes a whole look back as one step.

## Built in

Eight, Soft Sky first — the one the app comes with. Seven pair a built-in theme with a quiet set made for it; the eighth is the desktop through the glass, the way the first versions looked.

| Look | Page over backdrop | |
|---|---|---|
| **Soft Sky** | Midnight over Soft Sky | A cool page on a deep violet-blue night. The default. |
| **Slate** | Graphite over Slate | Warm text and a copper accent on the quietest grey. |
| **Deep Water** | Ocean over Deep Water | Blues and teals, deep. |
| **Pine** | Moss over Pine | Green-black, with a lime accent. |
| **Plum** | Dusk over Plum | Violet glass over violet folds, with a cyan accent. |
| **Umber** | Sepia Night over Umber | Warm darks, a terracotta accent. |
| **Linen** | Paper over Linen | A light page on warm, pale folds. |
| **Desktop** | Dusk over the desktop | The wallpaper, blurred, behind the glass. |

The quiet sets are three colours each, near each other on the wheel and kept deep — one sky, one water, one wood — rather than a spill of everything; the bolder sets from before are still under Behind the glass for anyone who likes them.

## Your own

Make the page look the way you want — a theme of yours or a built-in one, a set of colours of your own or Desktop, the frost and grain to taste — and press **Save this look…**. Name it, or take the suggested name (*Midnight over Soft Sky*), and it joins *Mine*: a tile like the others, in the View menu and the command bar with the others. **Save a copy…** on a built-in look is the way to start from one. **Delete** removes a look of yours; the theme and the backdrop it used stay.

## Sharing a look

**Export…** writes the look on screen — the named one, or the page as it stands — to a file, `Name.glassinelook.json`, with its theme and its backdrop inside it whole, so the file is everything the look needs whatever is on the other Mac. **Import…** reads one: the theme and the backdrop join *Mine* (unless a built-in one is the same, in which case that is used), the look joins *Mine*, and the page puts it on. A theme by itself still exports and imports under The page, for anyone who wants only that.
