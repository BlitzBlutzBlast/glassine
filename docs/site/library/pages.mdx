Most apps with more than one document open put a strip of tabs across the top of the window. Glassine keeps the window as quiet as it was and keeps the pages instead: a document you open stays open, as a page, until you close it, and the pages sit in a stack with the one you are writing on top.

## Moving between pages

Hold **⌥** and press **Tab**. The page steps back and the open pages fan out across it as cards — each the page itself in little, its title and its first lines in the theme's colours — in the order they were last used, the way ⌘Tab shows apps. The page before this one is already under the highlight.

- **Tab** steps along the row; **⇧Tab** steps back. ← and → do the same.
- **Let go of ⌥** and the highlighted page opens, sliding in as the other slides aside. So a *tap* of ⌥Tab flips to the page you were on before, and another tap flips back.
- **⌫** closes the page under the highlight. **Esc** leaves everything as it was. A click on a card opens it at once.

With one page open there is nothing to step through, and the pips in the footer give a small shake to say so.

## The pips

At the bottom of the page, beside the document's name, is a row of small pips: one a page, the one on screen lit, in the order they were last used. The pointer over one names it; a click goes there. More than nine pages show as nine and a count.

The [command bar](/reference/command-bar) (⌘K) lists every open page by name as well — *Page: …* — for a jump without the carousel, along with *Previous page* and *Close page*.

## Closing a page

**⌘W** (File → Close Page) closes the page on screen and brings up the one under it. Closing the last page leaves All Documents. A document moved to the Trash or shelved leaves the stack by itself; a renamed one follows its name.

The stack is kept between launches: quit with five pages open and they are open the next morning, the same one on top.

## One page at a time

Some people would rather have one document open, and nothing waiting behind it. Settings → Library → **Keep pages open**, off, does that: a document opened takes the place of the last, ⌥Tab does nothing, and ⌘W closes the document.
